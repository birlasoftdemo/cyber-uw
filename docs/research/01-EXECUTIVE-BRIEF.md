# Executive Brief — Cyber Underwriting Assistants

**Audience:** VP / leadership  
**Job:** Understand the opportunity  
**Read time:** ~5 minutes  
**As of:** September 2026  
**Companion pack:** [README.md](./README.md)

---

## The one-liner

Cyber insurance desks are being forced to quote **faster** in a soft market—while still defending **risk selection**. The opportunity is software that helps underwriters **reconcile what applicants claim vs what external signals show**, apply appetite rules, and leave an auditable decision—**not** another chatbot on a form, and **not** becoming the next Coalition.

## What we are *not* selling

- A risk-bearing cyber MGA (“next Coalition”)  
- A BitSight / CyberCube replacement  
- Autopilot that silently binds policies  

**We are enabling** MGA and carrier **desks**: intake → gap check → appetite → human bind → dossier → policy-system handoff.

---

## Opportunity shape (Estimated)

| Attractive | Less attractive soon |
| --- | --- |
| Mid-market specialty MGA / carrier cyber desks | Pure SMB straight-through already owned by Cowbell-class writers |
| Complex / OT industrial cyber (hours per file) | Reinsurer cat modeling (different category) |
| EU/UK books needing evidence packs (DORA / NIS2) | Chat-only copilots without gap + appetite + audit |

**Commercial wedge:** *speed without silent control concessions.*

**Illustrative spend:** mid desks processing hundreds–thousands of files/year can often justify **six-figure annual** platform spend if time-to-dossier and conflict-catch rates are proven—and PAS integration works. Exact WTP needs primary interviews (**Estimated**).

---

## Leadership decisions this brief supports

1. **Pursue** Decision Workbench as an **IP accelerator / enablement layer** for desks—not an InsurSec carrier.  
2. **Partner** ratings and cat models; **own** gap board, appetite, dossier, book alert, PAS adapters.  
3. **Fund** primary underwriter interviews (8–12) before scale—secondary research does not prove WTP.  
4. **Measure** time to decision-ready dossier, % attestation conflicts caught pre-quote, % decisions with auditable rationale.  
5. **Avoid** H1 chatbot-only demos as the proof of concept story.
