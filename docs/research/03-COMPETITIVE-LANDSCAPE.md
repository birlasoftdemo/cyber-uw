## 2. Positioning rules (locked)

| Stance | Examples | Implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Compete (workflow)** | Cytora-class, Concirrus Inspire, specialty AI UW, horizontal PAS AI modules | Overlap on intake/triage; differentiate on **cyber spine** (gap + appetite + book alert + dossier) |
| **Partner** | BitSight, SecurityScorecard, Black Kite, CyberCube, Cyence | Consume signals/models; mock in POC |
| **Substitute pressure (don’t clone)** | Coalition, At-Bay, Cowbell, Resilience, Corvus-by-Travelers | InsurSec capacity products raise the speed bar; wrong GTM for pure software IP |
| **Out** | Rebuild ratings engine; rebuild full cat model; become risk-bearing MGA | Scope kill |

**One-liner for GTM:**  
*We enable MGA/carrier desks to decide and defend cyber risks as fast as InsurSec writers—without rebuilding BitSight or becoming Coalition.*

**POC sell:** faster disciplined selection. **Do not sell:** “next Coalition.”

## 4. Battlecards (conversation aids)

### vs InsurSec (Coalition / At-Bay / Cowbell)

| They say | We say |
| --- | --- |
| “We already underwrite with live signals.” | “You’re capacity + product. We equip **your rivals’ desks** and **panel writers** who need the same discipline without changing their paper.” |
| “Why not just place with us?” | Different buyer. Desk tools vs risk transfer. |

**Win theme:** enablement, white-label, appetite personalization, PAS fit.  
**Lose if:** prospect wants to *become* an InsurSec MGA.

### vs BitSight / ratings alone

| They say | We say |
| --- | --- |
| “Just buy our score / portal.” | “Score is necessary, not sufficient. Underwriters still need appetite, RFIs, dossier, overrides, and PAS.” |

**Win theme:** orchestration + decision audit.  
**Partner ask:** data feed into gap board.

### vs Cytora / Concirrus / horizontal UW AI

| They say | We say |
| --- | --- |
| “We already digitize and route commercial risk.” | “Great on intake. Cyber needs **attest-vs-signal**, control floors, soft-market concession flags, and book concentration alerts.” |

**Win theme:** cyber-specific spine on top of (or beside) digitization.  
**Lose if:** prospect already standardized on their platform and only wants a thin cyber module—then partner/embed path may beat displace.

### vs “just add ChatGPT to the form”

| They say | We say |
| --- | --- |
| “We demoted extraction already.” | “Extraction is H1. Soft-market KPI is disciplined speed: gap board, versioned appetite, human bind, audit.” |

## 5. Competitive dynamics

| Force | Read | GTM implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Barriers | Domain connectors, trust/audit, distribution, signal COGS, AI governance | Lead with explainability + PAS story |
| Switching costs | High once appetite + adapters encoded; low for chatbots | Land with config + adapters; expand |
| Pricing pressure | Soft insurance line → sell hours saved + bad-risk avoided | ROI one-pager required |
| Consolidation | Travelers–Corvus pattern | Expect more tuck-ins; design for acquireability / PAS ecosystems |
| Substitutes | InsurSec + PAS AI modules | Highest threats to standalone SaaS |

### Indicators to watch

1. Marsh / Aon / Howden cyber rates & loss ratios  
2. New cyber logos for Cytora / Concirrus / DeNexus-class  
3. Carrier acquisitions of cyber tech / UW AI  
4. Supervisor language on aggregation + AI in UW  
5. Ratings vendors packaging deeper into UW workflows

## 6. Messaging / anti-messaging

### Say

- Speed **with** control discipline  
- Attest vs signal, then decide and defend  
- Agents assist · underwriter binds · every override is explainable  
- Partner ratings and cat models; own the decision spine  
- White-label / swappable appetite for client personalization  

### Don’t say

- “We’re the next Coalition”  
- “Fully autonomous underwriting” / silent auto-bind  
- “We replace BitSight / CyberCube”  
- Unverified mega-TAM slides as software market size  
- Fake primary UW quotes (secondary-only until interviews land)
