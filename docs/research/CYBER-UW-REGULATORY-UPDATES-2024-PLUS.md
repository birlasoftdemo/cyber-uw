# Cyber UW — Regulatory & Standards Updates (Jan 2024 → mid-2026)

**Purpose:** Durable planning input for Form Shipping Center / Typeform adaptive ingest / Confirm-Clear attestation.  
**Geography:** US, UK, EU (primary); India (light).  
**Evidence grades:** **A** primary law/regulator; **B** reputable industry/regulator commentary; **C** vendor/market blog.  
**Caveat:** Secondary research only. Primary carrier counsel and cyber UW interviews required before binding product or legal claims.

---

## 1. Executive summary

Since 2024, cyber underwriting intake has shifted from “checkbox controls” toward **evidence-backed, scope-precise attestation**. **EU DORA (17 Jan 2025)** and **NIS2 (apply from Oct 2024, uneven transposition)** pull ICT risk, IR testing, and third-party registers into broker dossiers for financial and essential entities. **NIST CSF 2.0 (Feb 2024)** and the **ISO 27001:2022 transition cutoff (31 Oct 2025)** change how apps and uploads are labelled. **US rescission practice** (MFA/control overclaim; 9th Cir. 2024 restatement) keeps misrepresentation risk Critical. **CPPA 2026 regs** tighten ADMT/cyber-audit expectations for CA-facing portals; **EU AI Act Art. 50 (2 Aug 2026)** requires chatbot transparency if assist chat ships. Product implication: frozen question text, Confirm/Clear, provenance, retention schedules, and transfer mechanisms for SOC2/policies—not free-text chat as sole disclosure.

## 3. Deep dives by theme

### 3.1 Insurance / cyber UW rules & guidelines

- **UK — PRA SS4/17 (cyber insurance underwriting risk), Nov 2024 update:** Board-owned affirmative + non-affirmative cyber strategy; appetite MI; aggregation stress (e.g. cloud) up to ~1-in-200. **Impact:** Carriers need structured, comparable control answers—not narrative dumps. **Grade A** — Bank of England / PRA SS4/17 PDF; PRA Rulebook SS4/17.
- **UK — Op resilience (31 Mar 2025):** In-scope firms must stay within impact tolerances for important business services. **Impact:** UW IR/BCP/recovery Qs for UK financial insureds should accept IBS/tolerance evidence language. **Grade A** — FCA Operational resilience; PRA 2025 insurance priorities letter.
- **US — Misrep/rescission:** Travelers/ICS (MFA attestation) remains the cyber landmark; 2024 appellate restatement (*Hughes*) keeps “material + false” sufficient in many jurisdictions regardless of intent. **Impact:** Scope-precise MFA/EDR/backup answers; Confirm/Clear; freeze question text. **Grade B** — Lockton; Insurance Journal; firm analyses of Hughes.
- **US — NAIC Insurance Data Security Model (#668):** ~28 jurisdictions by Aug 2025 (licensee security programs + breach notice)—primarily **carrier/licensee** duty, not insured questionnaire law, but raises regulator scrutiny of cyber books. **Grade A** — NAIC government-affairs brief (Aug 2025).
- **EU — EIOPA / DORA for (re)insurers:** Carriers themselves are DORA financial entities—own ICT evidence may inform vendor/portal diligence when shipping apps. **Grade A** — EIOPA DORA page; Reg. 2022/2554.

### 3.2 ISO / security standards

- **ISO/IEC 27001:2022:** IAF transition ended **31 Oct 2025**; 2013 certs invalid thereafter; full Stage 1/2 for returnees. Annex A themes (cloud, threat intel, supply chain, monitoring) align with modern app modules. **Grade A/B** — SGS; IAF Res. 2022-19 via CB guidance.
- **NIST CSF 2.0 (26 Feb 2024):** Adds **Govern**; catalog of informative references. Market apps increasingly ask CSF 2.0 alignment / governance owner. **Grade A** — NIST news + CSWP 29; mapping practice **Grade C**.
- **SOC 2:** Still evidence of control *operation*, not a substitute for answering carrier control Qs; privacy criteria useful for portal processors under CCPA/GDPR. **Grade B**.

### 3.3 Testing / assurance (pen test, vuln, IR exercise)

- **DORA Arts. 24–27:** Proportionate resilience testing for all in-scope FEs; **TLPT (Art. 26)** only for designated/systemic entities (often every 3 years). **UW impact:** Ask date, scope, findings closure—not binary “pen test yes.” **Grade A**.
- **NIS2 Art. 21:** Risk-management measures imply documented testing of IR and continuity; auditors ask for last exercise outcomes/remediation. Market UW blogs mirror this (**Grade C**); directive text **Grade A**.
- **UK CBEST / CQUEST:** Supervisory cyber assessment tools for regulated firms—signal that “tested recovery” language is expected. **Grade A/B** — FCA/PRA.

### 3.4 Personal data / privacy (portals uploading SOC2/policies)

- **GDPR/UK GDPR:** Purpose limitation, minimization, retention for security packs; Art. 28 processor terms for OCR/extract; Art. 22 if intake becomes solely automated UW decision. **Grade A**.
- **EU–US transfers:** DPF adequacy (Jul 2023) **does not cover many US insurers** (FTC/DoT jurisdiction requirement)—use **SCCs + TIA** (+ Art. 28). EDPB FAQ Jul 2024; GC upheld DPF Sep 2025. **Grade A**.
- **CPPA regs (eff. 1 Jan 2026):** Cybersecurity audits, risk assessments, **ADMT** rights; insurance clarification = CCPA applies to PI **not** covered by CA Insurance Code/IIPPA (e.g. marketing/analytics). **Grade A** — cppa.ca.gov.
- **US state privacy patchwork:** Multiple comprehensive state laws effective 2024–2026 (beyond CA)—retention/deletion and processor contracts for multi-state brokers. **Grade B**.
- **HIPAA:** Only if dossier includes PHI (e.g. healthcare insured)—BAA + minimum necessary; otherwise out of scope. **Grade A** (sectoral).
- **India DPDP:** Rules 13 Nov 2025; Consent Managers ~13 Nov 2026; substantive obligations ~**13 May 2027**. Light pilot note for India personal data in uploads. **Grade A/B**.

### 3.5 AI / digital ops affecting insurance

- **EU AI Act Art. 50:** From **2 Aug 2026**, disclose AI interaction (chatbots/agents); generative marking with limited transition to 2 Dec 2026 for some existing systems. EC guidelines **20 Jul 2026**. **Grade A**.
- **High-risk AI (Annex III):** Certain insurance pricing/risk assessment use-cases may be high-risk—**verify with counsel** before classifying intake prefill as high-risk ADM; keep HITL Confirm/Clear and non-binding proposals. **Grade A/B**.
- **CPPA ADMT:** Pre-use notice / opt-out / access for significant automated decisions (CA)—relevant if UW decisions are substantially automated. **Grade A**.

### 3.6 EU/UK financial & cyber resilience (evidence brokers must produce)

- **DORA:** ICT risk mgmt, major incident reporting, testing, third-party contractual clauses/registers—core dossier artifacts for EU financial insureds/intermediaries in scope. **Grade A** — EIOPA.
- **NIS2:** Broader sectoral essential/important entities; board accountability; supply-chain; reporting—drives evidence-style UW questions. Transposition incomplete in some MS as of 2025–26. **Grade A** — Commission digital-strategy; ECSO tracker **B**.
- **UK:** Op resilience + PRA cyber UW SS—parallel pressure on tested recovery and disciplined cyber books. **Grade A**.

## 4. Implications for Form Shipping Center / Typeform ingest / Confirm-Clear

Concrete product requirements (not UI):

1. **Frozen wording + versioned packages:** Shipping Center must ship a versioned question set (ID, exact text, effective date) so fair presentation / misrep defense maps 1:1 to attested values.
2. **Scope fields mandatory on Critical controls:** MFA/EDR/backup/patch answers require system scope, %, exceptions, as-of date—block binary overclaim.
3. **Confirm/Clear is the attestation boundary:** AI prefill = proposal only; attested set queryable; never auto-submit model output.
4. **Evidence cite-back:** Link field ↔ dossier page/clause (SOC2 TSC, ISO Annex A, pen-test exec summary date)—reports do not replace Q answers.
5. **Geo-adaptive modules:** EU financial → DORA/NIS2 evidence prompts (IR exercise date, ICT TPRM, major-incident process); UK financial → op-resilience/IBS language; US → MFA attestation patterns.
6. **Certification status enums:** ISO 27001 = `2022_valid | transition_programme | none | unknown` (no silent “certified” on lapsed 2013).
7. **Retention & transfer:** Configurable purge for declined vs bound; SCCs/Art. 28 for EU→US portal processors; insurers generally **cannot** rely on DPF self-cert alone.
8. **AI disclosure hook:** If assist chat enabled for EU users, Art. 50 notice at first interaction by Aug 2026; keep Pathway B form as sole truth store.
9. **ADMT/Art. 22 guardrail:** Intake assist must remain non-binding; human Confirm + UW referral—no solely automated bind/decline in v1.
10. **Attestor role capture:** Broker vs insured officer selectable per outtake (pilot lock)—store role, identity, timestamp.
