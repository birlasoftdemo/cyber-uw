FAQs

Ask us straight

Are you trying to be the next Coalition?
No. Coalition sells insurance and a security bundle. We sell a workbench for desks that keep writing their own paper: pull the pack, catch control gaps, apply appetite, hand off to PAS. Wiring in ratings and cat feeds is intentional. It is not “we gave up.”
Do we have to throw out BitSight or our PAS?
No. The tools that stick become the place everything meets: ratings and catastrophe models come in, policy admin gets the decision out. We live between broker email and your core. We do not replace either.
Will it bind on its own or invent answers on the app?
No. That is how you lose trust and get into regulatory trouble. Humans still quote, refer, or decline. What came from the applicant vs what came from a scan stays clear on the file, even when the broker is yelling for a same-day quote.
Who is this actually for?
Specialty mid-market MGA and carrier cyber desks that need speed without rebuilding an InsurSec stack. Complex or OT files where one submission eats hours. EU and UK books where evidence packs are not optional. Bad fit: SMB straight-through that Cowbell-class writers already own.
How is this different from a form chatbot?
A chatbot pulls fields out of a PDF. That is the easy demo. A workbench stages the job: show the control gaps, apply appetite with an override log, leave a decision file you can defend. In a soft market, selection discipline is the score. Shiny questionnaire extraction is not.
