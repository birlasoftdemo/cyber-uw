Do they still assemble the form by hand?

No — not universally. Digitally mature programs already ship a questionnaire: Marsh’s Online Cyber is a single application accepted by leading insurers. Coalition / At-Bay / Cowbell run their own portals (wrong GTM for us). Cytora-class tools extract whatever arrives.

Yes — still common on MGA / carrier desks we sell to. Specialty packages still land as email/PDF (SageSure’s six-step path). Cyber questions stay appetite-specific; no two portals match (RQB). Follow-up attachments and RFIs still explode cycle time.

Our Form Shipping Center is the carrier-side move: versioned modules for this appetite, tracked return, then Ideal vs Detected in the same record — not a claim that the market has no portals.

Second model (Accenture, no per-txn clock): 8 UWs × 35% non-core × 1,880 hrs = 5,264 admin hours/year. Reclaiming half of that (not promised) ≈ 1.4 FTE. Loaded at $130k × 1.4 ≈ $182k → ~$255k capacity before counting Ops.

Where we still win vs workflow AI

Intake extract is parity (Cytora-class). The wedge is cyber-specific: adaptive ship + track, attest vs signal, early RFI in one inbox, appetite Q/R/D, book watch, approve-before-send PAS — in one white-label spine.

Partner — do not rebuild

Ratings / ASM (BitSight-class) and cat / portfolio models (CyberCube-class). Consume the signal; alert concentration at quote time. Broker portals (Marsh) sit adjacent, not opposite.

Out of scope — wrong GTM

Not Coalition / At-Bay (they take insurance risk). Not a bound-premium engine. Agents assist; the underwriter binds.
