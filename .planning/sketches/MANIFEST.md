# Sketch Manifest

## Design Direction

Hybrid guided-agentic journey + polished UW workbench. Feel target from insurance/reinsurance submission UW (confidence, craft, hierarchy) — not a component port. Cyber stages keep attest-vs-signal / gaps / book jobs. Every stage advances only on Proceed; HITL where AI or Gap board work applies.

**Risk Analysis (2026-09-17 explore):** On-stage proof with citations — submission ↔ market and submission ↔ book capacity. Objective (no tension/bias flavor). Spatially distinct grammars (comparative vs ledger). Shared cite slide-in for deeper references only. See `.planning/notes/risk-analysis-evidence-composition.md`.
