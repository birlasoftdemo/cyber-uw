# Risk Analysis evidence composition

James needs to *see* proof — with references — that ties this submission to two places:

1. **Risk in the market** (peers, industry, what’s normal outside this file)
2. **Risk capacity on his book** (limit, ALE, what binding does to the bottom line)

Not a prettier KPI grid. Not a score that winks “bind” or “decline.”

## What we decided

| Topic | Decision |
|-------|----------|
| Tone | Instrument, not advisor. No tension framing. No good/bad cheerleading. |
| On stage | **Both** bridges visible. Two regions. |
| How they differ | **Spatial** grammar — market as comparative space; capacity as ledger space. Same calm palette. |
| Slide-in | **One shared panel.** Deeper cite-backs only. Same chrome whether market or capacity opened it. |
| What the slide-in is *not* | A parking lot for the four KPI tiles. Those belong on stage (or get replaced by the new spatial regions). |

## Copy locks (desk)

- Drop **Active** on the stage title (and the uppercase eyebrow habit on this surface).
- Rename **Security posture** → **Security rating**. Kill the redundant “partner rating / security rating” double-label.

## Why the old UI fails the job

The Industry / vs industry / Percentile / Cohort tiles all wear the same suit. Same card, same eyebrow, green “+49.” That *flavors* the data and doesn’t connect submission → market → book. Capacity lives somewhere else on the page (ALE charts) with no shared citation trail.

## Next design move

Sketch **geometry**, not drawer shopping:

- Market region: comparative scale / peer space
- Capacity region: ledger / limit math
- Shared cite panel: source trail only

Document-review and screenshot tiles still need their own evidence formats — planted as a seed until this composition lands.
